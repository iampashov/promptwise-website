Project Introduction

What is PromptWise?

PromptWise: Writing for the Planet in the Digital Era is an Erasmus+ Mobility of Youth Workers project that brings together 36 youth workers from Germany, Greece, Portugal, Czechia, Italy, North Macedonia, Romania, Spain and Türkiye to explore AI literacy, responsible digital practices and environmental sustainability.

The project addresses a growing gap in youth work: while AI tools are becoming mainstream, most practitioners lack the skills to use them critically, responsibly and sustainably.

Participants are empowered through non-formal education methods such as hands-on workshops, prompt-writing labs, reading circles, field interviews, creative campaigns and a study visit. They gain practical skills in AI literacy, prompt engineering, sustainable digital habits, algorithmic bias detection and project design.

Coordinated by VMDO – Verbund sozial-kultureller Migrantenvereine Dortmund e.V. (Germany) and funded by the Erasmus+ Programme of the European Union.

Why We're Here

Project Objectives

Four competence areas every participant works on during the training and carries back into daily youth work.

AI Literacy

Improve participants’ ability to use Artificial Intelligence tools critically, safely and responsibly within their youth work practice. Learn what AI can and cannot do, how to identify bias, and how to apply AI ethically.

Prompt-Writing Competence

Develop practical prompt-engineering skills that participants can apply immediately: designing effective prompts for session planning, creating learning materials, and adapting AI outputs to specific youth groups.

Sustainable Digital Habits

Connect AI use to its environmental impact. Understand how AI systems consume energy, water and raw materials, and commit to reducing digital waste through concrete, measurable behaviour changes.

Inclusive & Learner-Centred Methods

Strengthen participants’ capacity to integrate digital tools in ways that are inclusive, accessible and learner-centred, so that technology serves people from all backgrounds, including those with fewer opportunities.

What We Explore

Project Activities

The building blocks of the programme, from the first prompt to the final campaign.

PromptWise Toolkit

Co-creating an Open Educational Resource for youth workers: modules, prompt templates, reflection tools and sustainability guidelines.

Prompt Writing

Designing, testing and refining prompts for youth work purposes: context, task, format, tone and ethics.

Bias & Digital Citizenship

Identifying and addressing algorithmic bias in AI systems and practising critical, ethical use.

Digital Sustainability

Exploring the environmental footprint of AI (energy, water, e-waste) and how to minimise it.

“One Prompt, One Planet”

A creative awareness campaign made by participants and tested with local people.

Erasmus+ & Youthpass

Discovering the programme, reflecting daily on learning and recognising competences with Youthpass.

Our Journey

What We'll Do

8 activity days plus 2 travel days of hands-on learning, intercultural exchange and digital skill-building in Istanbul.

01
Arrival

Arrival in Istanbul

Participants arrive from all nine countries, check in at the hotel and meet the team over dinner.

02
Day 1

Welcome & Getting to Know

Opening of the training, name games, team building, mapping expectations, ground rules, the Erasmus+ world café and a city hunt. Welcome night.

03
Day 2

Exploring AI

“How AI works in simple terms”, Step Forward If…, the Human vs. Machine game, national realities survey and our digital footprints. Intercultural night 1.

04
Day 3

Anatomy of a Good Prompt

Snowball discussion, the prompt-writing workshop, reading circles on sustainable AI, theatre plays and an eco-audit walk. Intercultural night 2.

05
Day 4

Eco-Digital Practices

Reducing data waste and greener cloud habits, Prompt Studios with dot-voting, study visit to Deneyap Atölyeleri and mid-term evaluation.

06
Day 5

AI Literacy & Storytelling

Jigsaw learning on AI literacy, the simulation game “The Algorithm Decides”, prompt and context engineering, digital storytelling and a dinner in nature.

07
Day 6

One Prompt, One Planet

Designing campaign materials, testing them with local people and street interviews, guest speaker on green AI, movie night.

08
Day 7

Project Factory & Design Sprint

New Erasmus+ project ideas, dissemination planning and a design sprint to finalise the PromptWise Toolkit and the Workshop Replication Plan.

09
Day 8

Evaluation & Youthpass

What have we done together, final evaluation, the Youthpass ceremony and the goodbye evening.

10
Departure

Journey Home

Final goodbyes and departure from Istanbul with new skills, friends and a plan for the follow-up at home.

Every day has two coffee breaks (11:30 and 16:30), a daily reflection session at 18:00 and an evening programme.

Expected Results

What You Take Home

By the end of the PromptWise training, every participant will have made measurable progress across four areas of competence, during and after the activity.

A solid understanding of how AI tools work, including their capabilities, limitations and ethical implications.

Practical prompt-engineering skills: the ability to write, test and refine prompts for a wide range of youth work purposes.

Knowledge of AI’s environmental footprint (energy consumption, water usage, e-waste) and how to minimise it.

Awareness of algorithmic bias and strategies to counteract it in your professional practice.
